Classroom resources

A growing range of classroom resources, built to put the book's vocabulary into everyday practice.

Designed for ELSAs, SENCOs, and teachers who want an easy way to bring emotional literacy into daily classroom life.

Emotion Word Posters

Classroom display posters, one for each emotion word, in two versions:

  • Word and illustration only (great for Early Years settings)
  • Word, illustration and written definition (perfect for older children)

Each version is available in A3 and A4.

These posters are ideal for word walls and display boards, for example to prompt 'word of the week' discussion and activities.

Available as digital downloads via TES Resources.

Print on demand option — coming soon.
